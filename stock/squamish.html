Squamish Stock Photos

Squamish sits at the head of Howe Sound, halfway between Vancouver and Whistler. The granite of the Stawamus Chief, Shannon Falls, the Mamquam River and miles of forest trail make it one of the best outdoor towns in Canada for climbing, mountain biking, highlining and whitewater. These are original stock photos of Squamish and Howe Sound by local photographer Jeremy Allen.
