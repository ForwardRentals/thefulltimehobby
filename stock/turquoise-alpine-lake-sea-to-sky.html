Turquoise Alpine Lake, Sea to Sky

Location: Panorama Ridge, Garibaldi Provincial Park, BC

A lone hiker stands on a rocky outcrop high above the milky turquoise water of Garibaldi Lake, with glaciated peaks and summer clouds filling the horizon.

Turquoise Alpine Lake, Sea to Sky: an original landscapes stock photo by photographer Jeremy Allen, taken at Panorama Ridge, Garibaldi Provincial Park, BC in August 2017. Shot on the Panorama Ridge hike, one of the classic day trips in Garibaldi Provincial Park.
