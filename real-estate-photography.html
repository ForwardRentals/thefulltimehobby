Service area

Real estate photography across the Sea to Sky corridor

One photographer from the Howe Sound fjord to the Pemberton Valley. Each town sells differently, so each shoot does too.

Squamish

From new townhome and condo developments in Garibaldi Highlands and downtown to family homes in Brackendale, Valleycliffe and Dentville, Squamish listings sell on the Chief, the Tantalus Range and easy access to everything. I live here, so I know the light on every side of the valley.

Whistler

Chalets in Alpine Meadows and Emerald, ski-in/ski-out in Blackcomb Benchmark and Kadenwood, Creekside and Village condos, and Function Junction homes. Whistler buyers want to see the mountain, the ski access and the rental potential, and I shoot to show all three.

Pemberton & Mount Currie

Acreage, farmland, Pemberton Meadows and One Mile Lake. Big lots are where drone aerials do the most work, showing the land, the outbuildings and Mount Currie behind. I pair interior stills with aerials for properties that need both.

Lions Bay, Britannia Beach & Furry Creek

Oceanfront and view homes on the Howe Sound side of the highway, where the water, the islands and the road down the fjord are the selling points. A quick drive from Squamish, with no ferry or bridge to cross.

Also available in Vancouver and on the North Shore by arrangement. Not sure if your property is in range? Just ask.

Add aerials and show the whole picture

A Sea to Sky property sits in a setting: the mountain behind it, the river or fjord beside it, the trail at the end of the street. A drone photo shows that in one frame, and it’s often the image that gets the listing shared.

  • Lot and acreage — boundaries, outbuildings and how the land lies

Questions

Real estate photography FAQ

What areas do you cover for real estate photography?

I'm based in Squamish and shoot listings across the Sea to Sky corridor: Squamish, Whistler, Pemberton, Lions Bay, Britannia Beach, Furry Creek and Mount Currie. I also work in Vancouver and on the North Shore by arrangement.

How much does a real estate photo shoot cost?

Pricing depends on the size of the property and what you want covered: interior photos, exteriors, drone aerials, video, or all of it. Send me the address, the square footage and your timeline and I'll send a clear quote. Because I'm already in the corridor, there's no Vancouver-based travel to build into it.

How long does a shoot take?

A typical condo or townhome takes an hour or two. Larger homes, acreage and multi-unit new builds take longer, and I'll tell you what to expect when I quote. I work room by room, so the home is never tied up longer than needed.

How fast do I get the photos?

Tell me your listing date when you book and I'll work back from it. Photos are delivered in a private online gallery with one-click Download All, so you and your client can grab everything at once.

Can you include drone photos with the interior shots?

Yes. Aerials are one of the best ways to sell a Sea to Sky property: they show the lot, the view, the neighbours and the mountains behind. See my drone photography page for examples. Flights depend on airspace, weather and land access, and I'll check each before the shoot.

Do you photograph new builds, show homes and developments?

Yes, and it's a big part of my work. The photos on this page are from a new-build show home in Garibaldi Highlands, Squamish. For developers I can also do exterior and aerial marketing images, view studies from the height of each floor, and construction progress photography.

Do I need to be at the property during the shoot?

It's helpful if the owner or agent is there to open up, but not required. I can arrive with a lockbox code or key. Please make sure the home is ready before I arrive (see the checklist below).

How should I get my home ready for photos?

Declutter counters, put away personal items, open all blinds and turn every light on, and clear cars from the driveway. I've written a full room-by-room guide: how to prepare your home for real estate photos.

Can you also shoot video walkthroughs or social clips?

Yes. I'm a filmmaker as well as a photographer, so a listing video, a vertical cut for Instagram and Reels, or a drone flyover can be added to the same booking. See my motion work.
