Property photography for Britannia Beach & Furry Creek

This stretch of the Sea to Sky is small, scenic and easy to overlook, which is exactly why good photos matter. Listings here compete with Squamish and the North Shore, and what sets them apart is the setting: Howe Sound out the window, the mountains behind, and the sense of being just far enough from the city.

Properties range from older Britannia Beach homes with real character to newer communities at Furry Creek, so each shoot starts with working out what the buyer is really buying.

Drone photography in Britannia Beach & Furry Creek

Aerial photos suit this part of the corridor: the road, the rail line, the water and the mountains all fit in one frame, and they explain the location instantly to out-of-town buyers. As always, wind off Howe Sound, airspace and access are checked before a flight, and I’ll let you know what’s feasible at your address.

Nearby attractions like the Britannia Mine Museum and the provincial parks along the highway also mean extra sets of rules and visitors to consider on a drone shoot.
