Questions

Event photography FAQ

What types of events do you photograph?

Charity galas, award nights, conferences, AGMs, golf tournaments, fundraisers, art shows, product launches, brand activations and staff parties, from 30 guests to several hundred.

Where do you cover events?

Vancouver, the North Shore, Squamish, Whistler and Pemberton, plus resorts and venues across BC. I’m based in Squamish, so Whistler and Sea to Sky events have no extra Vancouver travel to build in.

Can you deliver photos during or right after the event?

Yes. I can send a set of quick social selects the same night or the next morning, then the full edited gallery after. Ask about it when you book.

Do you photograph sponsors and signage?

Yes. Sponsor booths, banners, hole signs and logos get dedicated coverage, so you have the photos you need for sponsor reports and thank-yous.

Can you also shoot video of the event?

Yes. I can film a short recap, speaker highlights or a sponsor reel alongside the photos. See my videography page.

Can you do headshots at the event?

Yes. A pop-up headshot station is a popular add-on at conferences and team events. See headshots.

How are photos delivered?

In a private online gallery with one-click Download All, with web and print sizes, ready to share with your team, sponsors and attendees.
