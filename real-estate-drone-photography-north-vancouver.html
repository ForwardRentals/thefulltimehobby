Drone photography in North Vancouver

On the North Shore, drone flights take planning. The lower areas sit close to Vancouver Harbour airspace, parks like Lynn Canyon and Mount Seymour have their own rules, and many neighbourhoods are tightly built. I check airspace, land access and privacy before booking an aerial, and I’ll tell you honestly what’s possible.

Where flights are feasible, aerials help show tree cover, lot shape and the view toward the water and city. For details on rules see my Sea to Sky drone guide.
