Property photography for Whistler

A Whistler buyer is often shopping from somewhere else, so the photos have to do the work of a showing. That means interiors that show the mountain through the window, ski rooms and mudrooms that look like selling points, and exteriors that show how the property connects to the trails and the lifts.

Season matters here. A winter listing needs snow cleared and the glow of a warm interior against a blue-hour sky; a summer listing wants the greenery, the deck and the bike-park energy. I’ll talk through timing with you so the photos match the market you’re selling into.

Drone photography in Whistler

Aerials are a powerful way to sell a Whistler property: they show the lot, the forest, the lifts and the peaks behind it. But the resort area is busy, with crowds, events and aircraft, so drone flights here need more planning than most places. I check airspace, bystander distance and landowner or strata permission first, and I’ll tell you honestly if a spot isn’t feasible.

Alpine weather also changes quickly. Wind and cloud can close a shoot window, so I plan backup times where I can. Read my guide to drone rules in the Sea to Sky.

Questions

Whistler real estate & drone photography FAQ

Do you shoot in Whistler Village and Creekside?

Yes, including condos and strata properties. I’ll check building and strata access rules ahead of time.

When is the best time to photograph a Whistler listing?

It depends on the season and what you’re selling. Winter wants snow cleared and good light; summer wants greenery and open decks. I’ll advise when you book.

Can you fly a drone near the Village or lifts?

Sometimes, but resort areas bring crowds, aircraft and extra rules. I confirm feasibility first and tell you honestly if it won’t work.

Do you photograph short-term rental properties?

Yes. Hot tubs, ski rooms, bunk rooms and views are what bring bookings, and I shoot to show them.

Can you combine Whistler with a Pemberton or Squamish shoot?

Yes. Grouping properties on one trip is efficient, and I’m happy to quote them together.
