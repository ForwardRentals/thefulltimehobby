Finch Drive Townhomes, Squamish: Real Estate Photography & Drone Aerials

Show home interiors, street-level exteriors and drone aerials for the Finch Drive townhomes in Squamish’s Garibaldi Highlands, photographed for Diamond Head Development. Browse the homes and community at finchdrive.com.

See the Photos Get a Quote

The project

A new townhome community above the Squamish valley

Finch Drive is a master-planned community by Diamond Head Development, a Squamish builder and developer, on the hillside above the valley. I photographed the townhome show home, the building exteriors and the whole site from the air, so buyers, agents and the sales team have a complete set: interiors for the listing, exteriors for the street view, and aerials that show the forest, the mountains and the setting.

01

Show home interiors

Open-plan main floor, kitchen, bedrooms, bath, garage and the mechanical room, shot level and bright with the forest views kept in the window.

02

Exteriors

The green panel cladding, wood soffits and sloped rooflines photographed from the street, row by row.

03

Drone aerials

Top-down site views, the rows of townhomes with their solar panels, and the valley and mountains behind. See my drone photography.

Interiors

Inside the Finch Drive townhome show home

Bright, honest interiors that sell the finishes without hiding the setting: oak floors, a long island kitchen, and windows that frame the evergreens and the rock face outside.

Exteriors & aerials

Finch Drive from the street and from the air

The aerials show what a floor plan can’t: how the community sits in the forest above the valley, with Garibaldi and the Squamish range behind it.
