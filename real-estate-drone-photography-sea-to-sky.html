Why one photographer for the whole corridor

The Sea to Sky is a single market in many ways: agents list in several towns, developers build in more than one, and buyers compare Squamish, Whistler and Pemberton in the same search. Booking one photographer who knows the light, the weather and the rules in all of them keeps your marketing consistent and your logistics simple.

Questions

Sea to Sky photography FAQ

What areas does the Sea to Sky corridor include?

The Sea to Sky is the stretch along Highway 99 from Horseshoe Bay in West Vancouver, through Lions Bay, Britannia Beach, Furry Creek and Squamish, up to Whistler, Pemberton and Mount Currie. I cover all of it, with Squamish as my base.

Can I book several properties on one trip?

Yes. Grouping properties by area keeps the cost down. Whistler and Pemberton, or Lions Bay, Britannia Beach and Squamish, are easy combinations.

Do you work in Vancouver and the North Shore?

Yes, by arrangement. I travel from Squamish for Vancouver, North Vancouver and West Vancouver bookings.

How does winter weather affect shoots?

Highway 99 can be affected by snow, rain or incidents, and weather affects aerials. I build in buffer time and will tell you early if a reschedule makes sense.

What if I have both a property and a project in the corridor?

That’s a great fit. I can shoot listing photos, construction progress, view studies and marketing video for the same client across towns.

Where’s your property?

Send me the address and your timeline. I’ll get back to you with a quote and available dates.
