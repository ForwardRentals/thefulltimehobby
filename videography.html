Questions

Videography FAQ

What kinds of videos do you make?

Commercials, brand stories, product films, event recaps, documentary shorts, social campaigns and real estate walkthroughs.

Do you handle the whole production?

Yes. Concept, scripting, scouting, shooting, aerials, editing, colour, music licensing and sound mix, or any part of it if you already have a team.

Can you deliver vertical video for social?

Yes. Every project can be delivered in 16:9, 1:1, 4:5 and 9:16, with cut-downs for Instagram Reels, TikTok and YouTube Shorts. See short films & social.

Do you shoot drone footage?

Yes. Aerials are often part of the package. See my drone page.

Can you shoot photos and video together?

Yes. Combining stills and motion on one shoot saves budget and gives you a consistent look across every channel.

How long does a video take?

A simple event recap can be turned around in days. A brand film with concept development usually takes a few weeks. I’ll give you a schedule with the quote.

How much does a video cost?

It depends on crew, shoot days, locations and post-production. Send a brief and I’ll send options for different budgets.
