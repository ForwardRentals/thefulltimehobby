Questions

Commercial photography FAQ

What kind of commercial photography do you do?

Mostly outdoor, lifestyle and sport brands: campaign imagery, product-in-use lifestyle photos, athlete portraits, event activations and social content. I also shoot hospitality, tourism and local-business brand libraries.

Can you shoot photo and video on the same day?

Yes. I’m a photographer and filmmaker, so a single shoot can deliver campaign stills, a hero brand film and vertical social cuts. See my videography work.

Do you help with locations, talent and permits?

Yes. I live in Squamish and know the Sea to Sky terrain well, from granite walls and alpine lakes to bike parks and beaches. I can scout, suggest athletes and talent, and plan around permits and park rules.

How is usage and licensing handled?

Every quote spells out usage: web and social, paid advertising, print, territory and term. You get exactly the rights you need, and extended licensing can be added later.

Where are you based and do you travel?

I’m based in Squamish, an hour from Vancouver and 45 minutes from Whistler. I regularly shoot across BC and travel further for the right project.

How much does a commercial shoot cost?

It depends on crew, days, deliverables and licensing. Send a brief, even a rough one, and I’ll send a clear estimate.

How are files delivered?

Edited selects are delivered in a private online gallery with one-click Download All, in web and full-resolution sizes, ready for your team, agency and printers.
