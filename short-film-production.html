Questions

Short film & social video FAQ

What counts as a short film?

Anything from about one to fifteen minutes with a story at its heart: an athlete profile, an adventure, a brand origin story, a community project or a festival film.

What is short-form social video?

Vertical videos for Instagram Reels, TikTok and YouTube Shorts, usually 7 to 60 seconds. They’re designed to hook in the first second and work with the sound off.

Can one shoot produce a film and social clips?

Yes, and it’s the most efficient way to work. I plan the shoot so the hero film and a batch of vertical clips come from the same days.

Do you make films for athletes?

Yes. Athlete edits and profile films for sponsors, social and film festivals are a big part of what I do. See sports & action.

Can you help with the story if we only have an idea?

Yes. Many projects start as one sentence. I’ll help develop the story, plan interviews and shape it in the edit.

Do you do monthly social content?

Yes. A regular shoot day each month can keep your channels stocked with fresh vertical content. Ask about ongoing packages.
