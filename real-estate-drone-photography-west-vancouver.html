Property photography for West Vancouver

West Vancouver listings are often about views of the water, the islands and the city skyline, plus homes built to take advantage of them. That means big windows, bright exteriors and tricky contrast between a glowing view and a darker interior.

I’m a Squamish photographer who drives past Horseshoe Bay all the time, and I cover West Vancouver by arrangement. If your property is part of a larger project or you want photo, drone and video together, it’s a convenient fit.

Drone photography in West Vancouver

Aerials suit West Vancouver’s steep, water-facing lots, but flights come with caveats: airspace around Vancouver’s harbour and airport, flying over or near water, strong winds on exposed slopes, and the privacy of close neighbours. I check each of these, and permissions, before booking.

If a drone isn’t feasible at a given address, I’ll say so and suggest alternatives like elevated deck-height photography. See my drone photography page for the full approach.

Questions

West Vancouver real estate & drone photography FAQ

Do you cover West Vancouver and Horseshoe Bay?

Yes, by arrangement. I’m based in Squamish and travel south for West Vancouver bookings.

How do you photograph a home with a big view?

I balance the exposure so the water and sky look natural through the windows while the interior stays bright and detailed.

Can you use a drone on the waterfront?

Often, with planning. Airspace, wind, flying over water and neighbour privacy all matter, and I confirm before booking.

Can you combine West Vancouver with Lions Bay or North Vancouver?

Yes. They’re close together and grouping them keeps costs down.

What do I need to do to prepare?

Follow the checklist in my home prep guide: declutter, clean windows, lights on and blinds open.
