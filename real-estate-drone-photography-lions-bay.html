Property photography for Lions Bay

Lions Bay is a small village on the Sea to Sky Highway, with houses stacked up the slope above Howe Sound. Almost every listing is about the outlook, and that creates a specific photography challenge: balancing bright water and sky against a darker interior without losing either.

I plan Lions Bay shoots around the time of day that gives the best light on the view, and I expose carefully so the water, islands and mountains look the way they do in person.

Drone photography in Lions Bay

From the air, a Lions Bay property reads clearly: the slope, the road, the water and the islands beyond. It’s a very effective way to show a steep lot and its view. Howe Sound winds can be strong, so I watch the forecast and plan flights for calmer windows. I also check airspace, parks and neighbour privacy before flying.

If you’re also selling elsewhere in the corridor, I can group shoots to save time and cost. More on drone photography.

Questions

Lions Bay real estate & drone photography FAQ

Can you photograph a steep hillside property?

Yes. I use wide, level interiors and aerial shots that show how the lot sits on the slope.

How do you handle a bright ocean view in photos?

By balancing the exposure so the water and sky look natural through the windows without losing detail inside.

Do you cover Lions Bay and Britannia Beach together?

Yes, they’re close, and I can group them with Squamish or the North Shore.

What time of day is best for a Lions Bay shoot?

It depends on which way the view faces. I’ll recommend a window when you book.

Can you fly a drone in Lions Bay?

Often yes, with the right planning. Wind, airspace, parks and neighbours all matter, and I confirm each before the shoot.
