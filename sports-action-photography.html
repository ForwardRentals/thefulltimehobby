Knowing the sport makes the shot

Action photography is about timing and position. Knowing where a rider will land, how a skier will turn, or which hold a climber is reaching for is what separates a clean frame from a near miss.

Because I do these sports myself, I can get into position safely, communicate with athletes in their language and get the shot in fewer laps.

  • Fewer takes — athletes save their legs for the shot that matters
  • Safe positioning — in the bike park, on the rope or in the alpine
  • Sponsor-ready — clean product and logo placement

Questions

Sports photography FAQ

What sports do you photograph?

Mountain biking, road and gravel cycling, skiing and snowboarding, rock climbing, skateboarding, BMX, surfing, paddling and trail running, plus team sports and competitions.

Who do you shoot for?

Brands and their athletes, magazines, event organisers, coaches and individual athletes who need images for sponsors and social. Past clients include Red Bull, Teton Gravity Research, Arc’teryx and Canadian Cycling Magazine.

Do you shoot races and competitions?

Yes. I can cover races, comps and festivals for organisers, sponsors and media, with fast selects for social during the event.

Can you shoot in the backcountry?

Yes. I ski, bike and climb, and I’m comfortable carrying camera gear into the alpine. Avalanche terrain and technical objectives are planned with the athletes and guides, with safety first.

Do athletes get photos for their sponsors?

Yes. Athlete sessions are planned around what sponsors want: product visible, logos clean and a mix of action and portrait.

Can you film as well?

Yes. I shoot video too, from edits for social to full short films like Deep Summer and Dirt Diaries.

Are your action photos available to license?

Many are. Browse the stock library or ask about a specific image.
