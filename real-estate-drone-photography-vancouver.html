Property photography for Vancouver

I’m based in Squamish, about an hour north of Vancouver on Highway 99, and I travel to the city for bookings that suit it. That tends to be developers with projects in both the city and the corridor, agents selling a Vancouver home alongside a Whistler or Squamish property, and clients who want photo, drone and video from one person.

In a market as dense as Vancouver, the photos that stand out are the ones that show the lifestyle around the property: the light, the views, the neighbourhood. That’s the approach I bring, from years of shooting adventure and brand work across the region.

Drone photography in Vancouver

Drone work in Vancouver is more constrained than most of the corridor: much of the city sits in or near controlled airspace around Vancouver International and the harbour, and it’s densely populated. That means advanced operations, authorization through NAV CANADA, and extra lead time. I’ll tell you up front whether a flight is realistic and what it needs.

For some Vancouver projects the best option is to shoot interiors and exteriors in the city and add aerials where they’re feasible. For Sea to Sky projects, I can fly aerials much more easily. See my drone photography page.
