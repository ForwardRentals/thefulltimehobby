Questions

Wedding photography FAQ

Where do you photograph weddings?

I’m based in Squamish and shoot weddings across the Sea to Sky: Squamish, Whistler, Pemberton, Britannia Beach and Lions Bay, plus Vancouver and the North Shore. I’m happy to travel further for the right wedding; travel is quoted up front.

Do you shoot wedding video as well as photos?

Yes. I’m a filmmaker as well as a photographer, so you can book photos, a wedding film, or both. A film can be a short highlight cut, a longer story edit built around your vows and speeches, or vertical clips for Instagram. See my videography page.

How much does wedding photography cost?

It depends on how many hours you want covered, whether you add video, and travel. Send me your date, venue and rough guest count and I’ll send clear options with no surprises.

What happens if it rains?

It rains in the Sea to Sky. Some of my favourite wedding photos were shot under clear umbrellas on a wet dock. I scout covered spots ahead of time and plan a backup for portraits, so weather never ruins the day.

Do you photograph elopements and small weddings?

Yes. Elopements and small weddings in the mountains are some of the best days to shoot: a lookout, a lake, a trail, or the top of the gondola. I can help with location ideas and timing for the light.

Do you offer engagement sessions?

Yes. An engagement session is a great way to get comfortable in front of the camera before the wedding, and the photos are ideal for save-the-dates.

How long until we get our photos?

You’ll get a handful of sneak peeks soon after the wedding. The full edited gallery is delivered in a private online gallery with one-click Download All, so you and your family can grab everything at once. I’ll give you a delivery date when you book.

Do you help with the timeline?

Yes. I’ll help plan the day around the best light, especially for sunset portraits, which are often the photos couples love most.
