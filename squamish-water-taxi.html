Squamish Water Taxi: Howe Sound From the Water

Squamish Water Taxi runs water taxi rides, boat rentals and wildlife tours on Howe Sound. These are my photos from out on the water with them.

Visit Squamish Water Taxi See the Photos

The business

A local captain, a fjord and the whole Sea to Sky corridor

Squamish Water Taxi offers water taxi service, boat rentals, guided wildlife tours and charters on Howe Sound, serving Squamish, Britannia Beach, Lions Bay and Vancouver. You can even plan a trip and get a fare estimate on their route map.

Howe Sound is one of the best places to see the Squamish landscape: the Stawamus Chief, the Tantalus and Garibaldi ranges and the long fjord walls look completely different from the water.
