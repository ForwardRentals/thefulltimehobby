Squamish & Whistler Stock Photos

I live and shoot in Squamish, so this library covers the Sea to Sky from the inside: the Stawamus Chief and Howe Sound, Garibaldi Provincial Park and the Black Tusk, Whistler's backcountry, Pemberton's valleys and the coast out to Tofino.

Tourism boards, outdoor brands, real estate marketers, publishers and local businesses use these photos for websites, campaigns, brochures and editorial features.

British Columbia Adventure Photography

Beyond the corridor there's backcountry skiing in the Coast Mountains, glaciers near Bella Coola, the Athabasca River in the Rockies, and mountain biking, climbing, highlining and kayaking across BC.

Every image is shot by me, Jeremy Allen, and licensed directly by me. You won't pay agency fees, and you'll always know who took the photo.

How do I buy a license?

Open any photo and tap License This Photo. That starts an email with the photo ID filled in. Tell me how and where you'll use it, and I'll reply with an invoice and the full-resolution file.

Do photos with people have model releases?

Releases vary by image. Landscapes are ready for any use. If a photo shows a person, ask me before using it in advertising and I'll confirm what's available.

Can I use these photos for free with credit?

No. All images are © Jeremy Allen / The Full Time Hobby, and every use needs a license. The watermarked previews on this page are for browsing only.

Looking for something that isn't here?

This is only part of the archive. Send me what you're after, whether that's a trail, a peak, a season or a sport, and I'll dig through the library. I can also shoot it for you.
